# IDEAS
## Refactoring & Re-architecting
- Rewrite the player control logic to optionally interpret inputs as "tilting the gravity vector" relative to the camera instead of applying torque to the ball directly.
- Overhaul the GUI to include a robust "Party Mode" menu for accessing SMB-style minigames.
- Implement an event-driven Boss system in TorqueScript.

## Feature Expansions
- **Monkey Target:** A minigame where players roll down a giant ramp, open their ball like a glider, and land on targets.
- **Monkey Bowling:** Using the marble physics engine to bowl down pins.
- **Balance Board / Gyro Support:** Integrate controller gyro (Switch/DualShock) to emulate the "Step & Roll" balance board experience.
- **Multiplayer:** Co-op story mode and competitive racing (Monkey Race).
